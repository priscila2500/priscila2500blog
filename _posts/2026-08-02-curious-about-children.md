Why I'm now curious about having a child

1. My grandmother died November, 2025. This made me reconsider the passage of time. I may be carrying unprocessed grief about this.  
2. I asked myself "If  mom where to die, what would happen to me then?"
3. I like the idea of a child knowing my mom so that we can remember her together once she's gone, hopefully in a very long time from now. I like the idea of a child sharing memories with me and helping connect my past and my future. 
4. I feel bored and unsatisfied with my job and I may be looking at family and relationships to relieve me of boredom and lack of meaning.  
5. I would be curious about the child. I would want to support and parent them. I would want to get to know them.
6. Sometimes I walk around my apartment talking to an invisible baby. This seems to have evolved from the self love stuff I started to do with my therapist, talking to myself in my apartment and asking how I was feeling and cheering myself on to do things such as wake up, clean, go for a walk, etc. 
7. A child will be a lot of work and effort and in the past I shied away from the though of it. I've had plenty of rest and downtime, being in this apartment on my own for the last six years. I've come around to understand that important, meaningful things require effort. Writing a book, going to the gym, all kinds of important things require effort.
8. A child seems like an intensive investment the first fifteen years. I would still have time to write and for my hobbies down the line, I think. 


Things that I remain unsure about

1. I may be seeing having a child as an avenue to receiving more connection and support over time. Not from the child specifically, but from other members of my family and maybe society and other people. 
2. What I fear the most is being alone with too much responsibility. What I seem to want the most is to have more connection and support than I currently have. 
3. Having a child might be neutral to having more connection and support. It seems you could be a parent and be alone and overwhelmed or be childfree and have connection and support. And vice versa.
4. What I may really be puzzling over is how to obtain more connection and support. 
5. Related to this might be that I grew up in a home without a lot of connection or emotional support. I wonder if there is an impossibility to me having that environment in adulthood because I did not have that family and I don't know how to create it.
